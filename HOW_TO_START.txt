========================================================================
            FS_Chat - QUICK START & LAUNCH GUIDE
========================================================================

Welcome! This application is fully decentralized and designed for maximum 
ease of use — no terminal commands, no complex installation steps.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. FOR WINDOWS PC (EASIEST WAY):
------------------------------------------------------------------------
👉 Single executable file: "FS_Chat.exe"
   - Just double-click on "FS_Chat.exe".
   - The chat opens immediately in a standalone desktop window!
   - NO Node.js, Python, or additional runtime dependencies required.

👉 Browser Selection:
   - If you have multiple browsers installed (Chrome, Edge, Brave, Firefox, Opera),
     you can select your favorite browser in "Chat Settings" -> "Startup Browser".
   - The choice is saved in browser_config.json and applies automatically.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. FOR SMARTPHONES (ANDROID & IPHONE / IPAD):
------------------------------------------------------------------------
You do NOT need to download complex binary packages on your mobile phone!
The application is a Progressive Web App (PWA) that installs in 1 tap:

👉 For Android (Google Chrome):
   1. Open the chat link in Chrome.
   2. Tap the 3-dots menu (⋮) in the top right corner.
   3. Select "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
   4. The FS_Chat icon will appear on your phone home screen — tap it 
      to launch it as a full native app without browser URL bars!

👉 For iPhone / iPad (Safari):
   1. Open the chat link in Safari.
   2. Tap the Share button (square with arrow pointing up ⎋) on the bottom bar.
   3. Scroll down and select "Add to Home Screen".
   4. Tap "Add". The app icon appears on your home screen and launches 
      as a smooth, standalone iOS application!

👉 Voice calls & media on smartphones:
   - Hold your finger on the microphone button for Push-to-Talk voice chat.
   - All voice and audio messages are End-to-End encrypted over Nostr relays 
     WITHOUT sharing or exposing your real IP address!

------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. FOR APPLE MAC & LINUX:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
👉 For Apple Mac:
   - Double-click "FS_Chat.command".

👉 For Linux:
   - Run "FS_Chat.sh" (chmod +x FS_Chat.sh && ./FS_Chat.sh).

------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. PRIVACY & SECURITY:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- All direct messages use NIP-17 Gift Wrap (End-to-End Encrypted).
- Anonymous voice chat runs over Ephemeral Nostr relays (Kind 20000+),
  with NO peer-to-peer WebRTC IP leakage.
- No centralized user database; all data stays on your local device.
========================================================================
